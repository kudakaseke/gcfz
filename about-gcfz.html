About GCFZ

Improving cancer awareness and support for women in Zimbabwe.

We work to help women, families and communities find reliable information, seek screening early and navigate cancer care with dignity.

Mission

To promote awareness, early detection and compassionate support for people affected by gynaecological cancers across Zimbabwe.

Vision

A Zimbabwe where every woman has the knowledge, confidence and access needed to protect her health and receive timely care.

Our Work

Community education, screening advocacy, patient guidance, partnerships and practical support for caregivers and families.
