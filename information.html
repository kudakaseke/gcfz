Cancer information

Overview

This page covers basic cancer information for patients and families. A doctor or nurse can answer questions about your own health.

What to know

  • Ask a health worker about symptoms that are new or do not go away.
  • Your screening needs depend on your age, health and family history.
  • If you receive a diagnosis, ask what it means and what happens next.

Where to get help

Contact GCFZ for information about local programmes and patient support.
